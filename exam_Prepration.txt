1.What is the difference between computer organization and architecture?
  Computer architecture refers to those attributes of a computer system that are visible to a programmer — in other words, the attributes that have a direct impact on the logical execution of a program.
  Computer organization refers to the operational units and their interconnections that implement or realize the architectural specifications.
    
    Examples of architectural attributes include:
      the instruction set
      number of bits used to represent data types (numbers, characters, etc.)
      I/O mechanisms
